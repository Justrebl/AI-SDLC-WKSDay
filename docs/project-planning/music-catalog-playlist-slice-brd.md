## Executive Summary

A listener choosing music for a listening moment may need a way to collect tracks into a short listening list. This is a **hypothesis**, not a finding from customer research. The playlist slice lets a listener browse the Music Catalog and collect tracks into a single playlist during a session.

The expected value is that selected tracks stay together during a session, so the listener can see their choices without repeatedly scanning the full catalog. This benefit has **not yet been measured**.

Scope is deliberately small: one playlist, in-memory state only, and none of users, authentication, persistence, reorder, remove, search, or playlist creation. Every acceptance target in this document is **not yet demonstrated**. The BG-001 target (6 of 10 testers, by end of year) is agreed but not yet measured.

Source: the reviewed delivery brief at `docs/project-planning/playlist-design-decisions.md`.

## Business Goals

### BG-001: Browse and collect tracks in one playlist during a session

- Statement: A listener can browse the catalog and collect tracks in a single playlist during a session.
- Priority: MUST
- Agreed target (OQ-004): 6 of 10 testers add at least one track and report a positive experience, by end of year; owner: TPM (Julien). Positive = rating of at least 4 out of 5. Baseline: none (new feature). Draft questions: "Did you find you could have done without it?" and "Did you feel it brought something to your listening session?" Scoring: Q1 reworded as "How much did the playlist help?" (1-5); a tester is positive if the average of both ratings is at least 4. (OQ-003, OQ-004).
- Expected value (hypothesis, unmeasured): the listener's selected tracks stay together during a session, so they can see their choices without repeatedly scanning the full catalog.
- Assessment approach (agreed): post-session listener feedback from the 10-person test; two questions rated 1-5 (Q1 "How much did the playlist help?", Q2 "Did you feel it brought something to your listening session?"). Sentiment analysis is not adopted.

## Functional Requirements

- `FR-001`: A listener can browse the track catalog.
  - Actor: Listener. Trigger: Opens the application. Outcome: Catalog tracks are visible.
  - Acceptance Criteria: AC-001. Business Goal: BG-001. Priority: MUST.
- `FR-002`: A listener can add a catalog track to the single playlist.
  - Actor: Listener. Trigger: Chooses to add a track. Outcome: The track appears in the playlist, visible alongside the catalog.
  - Acceptance Criteria: AC-002. Business Goal: BG-001. Priority: MUST.
- `FR-003`: Adding a track that is already in the playlist is rejected with visible feedback.
  - Actor: Listener. Trigger: Adds a duplicate. Outcome: The playlist is unchanged and the listener sees feedback that the add was rejected. Feedback approach: visible inline message with role="alert" (OQ-006; confirm at the RPI plan gate).
  - Acceptance Criteria: AC-003, AC-004. Business Goal: BG-001. Priority: MUST.
- `FR-004`: An empty playlist shows a visible empty state.
  - Actor: Listener. Trigger: Views the playlist with no tracks. Outcome: An empty-state message is visible. The delivery brief's wording is "Your playlist is empty. Add a track to get started."
  - Acceptance Criteria: AC-005. Business Goal: BG-001. Priority: MUST.
